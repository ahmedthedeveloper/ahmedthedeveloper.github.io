Privacy policy

NEXUS · last updated 12 November 2026

NEXUS is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, who also runs it. This page explains what it does with Google user data.

What NEXUS accesses

  • Files in the owner's Google Drive that NEXUS itself created, through Google's drive.file permission. These live in a folder named NEXUS.
  • NEXUS cannot see any other files in the Drive, and it does not request access to email, contacts, calendar or profile data.

How the data is used

  • Only to keep the owner's project documents in sync between the owner's computer, the owner's server and the owner's Google Drive.
  • It is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

  • Copies of the synced files are kept on the owner's own computer and the owner's own server.
  • The Google access token is stored encrypted on the owner's server and is never sent anywhere except Google.

Removing access

The owner can revoke NEXUS's access at any time under Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. Deleting the NEXUS folder in Google Drive removes the files there.

Use of Google user data

NEXUS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

NEXUS

A personal multi-agent workspace. One person runs it on their own computer and their own server.

What it is

NEXUS coordinates a team of AI agents working on the owner's projects. It keeps project documents in sync between the owner's PC, the owner's server and the owner's Google Drive.

Who it is for

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and nobody else's data is involved.

How it uses Google Drive

  • It uses Google's drive.file permission, so it can only see and change files that NEXUS itself created.
  • It keeps those files in one folder, NEXUS, in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • It cannot see any other files in the Drive.

Privacy policy
